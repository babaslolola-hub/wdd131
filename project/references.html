References and Resources

FleetSafe's safety guidance is informed by the following recognised workplace safety resources. These sources provide general guidance and do not replace site-specific procedures or approved forklift operator training.

1. Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA)

Resource: Powered Industrial Trucks — Forklifts.

This resource explains common forklift hazards, safe operation, operator training, and ways to reduce workplace risks.

Read the OSHA forklift safety guidance

2. Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA)

Resource: Powered Industrial Trucks eTool.

This resource covers forklift operation, workplace conditions, equipment hazards, and operator training.

Explore the OSHA Powered Industrial Trucks eTool

3. Health and Safety Executive (HSE)

Resource: Introduction to Workplace Transport Safety.

This guidance discusses safe workplace traffic routes, separating pedestrians from vehicles, vehicle maintenance, and operator training.

Read the HSE workplace transport guidance

How these resources support FleetSafe

  • Pre-shift equipment inspections and defect reporting.
  • Safe forklift operation and load handling.
  • Pedestrian awareness and workplace traffic safety.
  • Operator training and appropriate defect follow-up.

Important note

OSHA and HSE guidance comes from the United States and United Kingdom respectively. Follow applicable Nigerian requirements and your workplace's approved safety procedures.
