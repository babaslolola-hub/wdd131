Forklift Safety Tips

Safe forklift operation protects operators, pedestrians, equipment and products. Follow these practices every shift.

1. Inspect Before Operating

  • Complete the pre-shift inspection checklist.
  • Check tyres, forks, brakes, steering, horn and lights.
  • Look for hydraulic leaks and visible damage.
  • Report defects before using the forklift.

2. Wear Required Safety Equipment

  • Wear the personal protective equipment required at your site.
  • Fasten the seat belt and use the operator restraint system.
  • Keep hands and feet inside the operator compartment.

3. Drive Carefully

  • Follow site speed limits and designated travel routes.
  • Slow down at corners, intersections and slippery surfaces.
  • Sound the horn where required and watch for pedestrians.
  • Maintain enough distance to stop safely.

4. Handle Loads Safely

  • Never exceed the forklift's rated load capacity.
  • Position forks correctly and secure the load.
  • Travel with the load lowered to a safe travelling height.
  • Do not carry passengers unless the forklift is designed for them.

5. Protect Pedestrians

  • Use designated pedestrian and forklift routes.
  • Never assume pedestrians have seen you.
  • Stop if your view is blocked and follow site rules for restricted visibility.
  • Never allow anyone to walk or stand beneath raised forks.

6. Report Defects Immediately

  • Stop operating if a defect makes the forklift unsafe.
  • Park in the designated safe area and lower the forks.
  • Report the defect to your supervisor or maintenance team.
  • Follow your site's isolation and repair procedures.
  • Do not return the forklift to service until it has been properly checked and authorised.

7. Park Safely After Use

  • Park only in an authorised location.
  • Lower the forks fully and place the controls in neutral.
  • Apply the parking brake and switch off the forklift.
  • Follow site procedures for charging or refuelling.

Remember

Inspect. Report. Verify. Operate safely.

Never operate a forklift you believe is unsafe. Stop and report the hazard according to your workplace procedures.
