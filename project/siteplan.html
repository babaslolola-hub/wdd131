Forklift Safety and Inspection Website Plan

Site Name

FleetSafe

FleetSafe is a website focused on forklift safety and inspection. The name combines "Fleet" and "Safe" to represent safe operation, inspection, and management of forklifts.

Site Purpose

FleetSafe will provide forklift operators and supervisors with safety information, pre-operation inspection guidance, and reminders for identifying equipment defects. The website will help users understand basic forklift safety practices and encourage proper inspection before operating equipment.

Scenarios

What should I check during a pre-operation forklift inspection?

What should I do if I discover a defect during a forklift inspection?

Color Schema

Dark Navy (#123047) will be used for the website header, main headings, and other important text to create a professional and trustworthy appearance.

Safety Yellow (#F4C430) will be used for safety warnings, inspection alerts, buttons, and important accents to make important information easy to notice.

Typography

Oswald will be used for the main heading and section headings. Its strong and clear appearance will help safety-related headings stand out.

Roboto will be used for body text and descriptions because it is clean, simple, and easy to read.
